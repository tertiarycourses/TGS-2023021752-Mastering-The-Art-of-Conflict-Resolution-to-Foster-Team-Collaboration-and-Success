# Activities — Mastering The Art of Conflict Resolution to Foster Team Collaboration and Success

Eight real-workplace simulations use fictional Singapore organisations. Every folder is self-contained and includes detailed instructions with scenario and role cards, a learner worksheet with questions and evidence fields, an observer/quality checklist, and an editable visual briefing deck.

Markdown files are paired with same-basename PDFs for printing and LMS use. Activities require no disclosure of personal conflicts and do not replace organisational policy, HR advice or legal advice.

- Activity 01: Conflict Signal Triage at LionCity Retail — Signal log · stage map · triage matrix
- Activity 02: Evidence Interview and Root-Cause Map at HarborLink Logistics — Interview guide · evidence matrix · root-cause map
- Activity 03: Conflict Style Choice Clinic at BrightWorks Digital — Style decision matrix
- Activity 04: Mediated Conversation at Mosaic Creative — Role cards · mediation record · observer checklist
- Activity 05: Interest-Based Negotiation at CarePoint Clinics — Negotiation canvas · option scorecard
- Activity 06: Leader Coaching Huddle at NovaTech Services — COACH planner · role cards · repair plan
- Activity 07: Policy, Ethics and Escalation at Meridian Hospitality — Policy-routing worksheet · evidence checklist
- Activity 08: 30-Day Resolution Review at GreenGrid Engineering — Review dashboard · prevention plan
