# Activity 05 — Interest-Based Negotiation at CarePoint Clinics

**Course:** Mastering The Art of Conflict Resolution to Foster Team Collaboration and Success (TGS-2023021752)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Negotiation canvas · option scorecard  
**Output:** An interest-based negotiation canvas, option scorecard and written service-allocation agreement.

## Objective

Negotiate workload and service priorities using interests, options and objective criteria.

## Psychological-safety boundary

Use only the fictional case. Do not require anyone to disclose a personal conflict. Any participant may call a pause or request another role. This learning activity does not replace organisational policy, HR advice or legal advice.

## Scenario

CarePoint Clinics must cover three Saturday vaccination sessions during a seasonal surge. Nurse lead Mei wants two extra staff at each session; operations lead Arjun says the overtime ceiling permits only one.
Both need safe patient care and sustainable staffing. Each receives private interests and tradeable options, then negotiates a package using demand data, skill coverage, overtime limits and rotation history.

## Detailed instructions

## Role cards

Give each participant only their own role before the first round. Observers use the checklist.

### Mei · Nurse Lead

**Goal:** protect patient safety and staff recovery.  
**Private brief:** Can trade session length and skill mix but not minimum clinical coverage.

### Arjun · Operations Lead

**Goal:** stay within approved cost and service commitments.  
**Private brief:** Can reallocate hours and seek conditional approval with evidence.

### Observer · Process Coach

**Goal:** test fairness and process quality.  
**Private brief:** Tracks questions, summaries, criteria, options and closing clarity.

1. **Prepare separately.** Each role identifies its position, underlying interests, evidence, constraints, preferred outcome, walk-away boundary and alternatives if no agreement is reached.

2. **Set a shared problem statement.** Convert the dispute into a joint question about meeting patient demand, staff capacity and service quality.

3. **Exchange interests.** Each side explains interests and constraints while the other paraphrases before responding.

4. **Agree objective criteria.** Select criteria such as patient risk, demand forecast, staffing capability, cost, equity and implementation time.

5. **Generate packages.** Create at least three multi-variable options rather than arguing about a single fixed allocation.

6. **Score options.** Rate each package against the agreed criteria, show assumptions and note where data is uncertain.

7. **Trade across differences.** Look for low-cost/high-value exchanges, sequencing, pilots and conditional commitments without trading away policy or safety.

8. **Check authority and fairness.** Confirm who can commit resources and whether affected staff or patient voices require consultation.

9. **Draft the agreement.** Record allocation, actions, owners, dates, evidence, communication and a review trigger.

10. **Plan no-agreement handling.** State the temporary operating rule and escalation route if authority or evidence is insufficient.

11. **Debrief process.** Identify which questions uncovered interests, which criterion resolved disagreement and what could damage implementation.

## Suggested timing

- 7 minutes — Role preparation
- 6 minutes — Shared problem and interests
- 7 minutes — Criteria
- 10 minutes — Option packages
- 8 minutes — Scoring and trade-offs
- 7 minutes — Agreement or escalation
- 5 minutes — Debrief

## Discussion questions

- What are each party's positions, interests, constraints and alternatives?
- Which objective criteria should guide a fair rota?
- What package trades across time, skill mix, session design and budget?
- What must be recorded so implementation does not create a new conflict?

## Submission

Submit the completed worksheet and checklist. Use role names rather than real personal data, mark unresolved information as unknown and retain the artefact only through the approved course channel.

## Debrief

The agreement explains interests, criteria, trade-offs, owners, escalation conditions and review date.
